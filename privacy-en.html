Routa Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Developer: BenderEdge (Yusuf Bender) · Contact: benderedge.app@gmail.com

This policy applies to Routa ("the app"), a mobile game developed by BenderEdge (Yusuf Bender). The app does not collect or sell your name, e-mail address, location or any other personal data.

Anonymous usage data (GameAnalytics)

To improve the game, we send anonymous game events to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark). GameAnalytics is a service provider that processes this data on our behalf. The events are:

  • session start and length,
  • level-ups,
  • where in-game currencies (gems, coins, fuel) come from and are spent,
  • tutorial steps, store visits, truck jobs,
  • error and crash reports,
  • device model, operating system version and game version.

The only identifier is an install number that the app generates randomly on first launch. It is not linked to any account, your name or your advertising ID. Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Turning it off: you can stop sending at any time with Settings → "Send anonymous usage data". Events waiting to be sent are then deleted from your device as well.

Deletion requests: to ask for deletion of data already sent, e-mail benderedge.app@gmail.com with your install number. You can find the install number in the draft e-mail opened by Settings → Send feedback.

GameAnalytics privacy policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your device:

  • your game progress (save file),
  • your settings,
  • a technical game log.

They are deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also delete your save with Settings → Reset.

Internet

The app uses the internet permission only to send anonymous usage data. Without internet the game keeps working; events are sent once a connection is available.

Purchases and ads

The closed beta has no real-money purchases and no ads. If Google Play Billing is used later, payments are processed by Google; the app never sees your card details. If ads are added, this policy will be updated first.

Feedback

Settings → Send feedback opens your mail app. The draft e-mail includes the game version, device model, system version, screen resolution, player level, play time and install number. Sending it is your choice; if you do not send it, nothing is shared. E-mails you send are used only to answer your feedback and fix bugs.

Permissions

  • Internet: anonymous usage data.
  • Vibration: button feedback.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
